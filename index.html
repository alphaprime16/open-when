A LITTLE SOMETHING I WROTE FOR YOU

Naya naya likha hai aapke liye kuch

Beginning with a hi and an awkward—
"Mujhe kutto se dar lagta hai."

Never knew I would be writing all this one day,
365 days turning into 730 days,
The feeling of strangers turning into home.

Been 2 years, but the feelings never faded,
The same butterflies coming right at me
Hearing you compliment me—
"Hayyeee, kitni pyari lag rahi meri baby."

I've always loved it and will always cherish it.
Your voice, your looks, your thoughts,
Your ideas, your smile, your eyes,
Have always just been something I adore so much in you.

You're really special to me.
Writing about you makes me feel like
How lucky I am to have you in my life. ♡

Love you so, so, so much, my babyyy. 💗
They say it's about the place, but with you,
It's just you and me enjoying ourselves.

You're really precious to me.
Take care of yourself for me. ♡
